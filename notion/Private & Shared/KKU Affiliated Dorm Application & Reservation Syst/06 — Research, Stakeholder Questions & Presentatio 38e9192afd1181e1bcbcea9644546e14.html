06 — Research, Stakeholder Questions & Presentation Kit

Purpose

Last revised: 2026-07-10

This page prepares research evidence, stakeholder questions, and presentation material for the accepted exact-room pilot of หอ 8 หลัง + วรอินเตอร์.

Research conclusion

The main opportunity is not simply “put the paper form online.” The product must connect:

  • exact rooms and room configurations;
  • roommate/whole-room occupancy;
  • early payment commitment;
  • SCB bulk QR payment documents;
  • payment results;
  • annual contracts;
  • renewal and resident changes;
  • later university handoff.

Validated facts

  • The two pilot dorm groups should be delivered together.
  • Room numbers are unique.
  • Applicants can choose exact rooms.
  • Normal capacity is two residents.
  • Whole-room rental is allowed.
  • Roommate grouping is required for shared rooms.
  • Leader sends invitation; both profiles already exist.
  • Roommate accepts relationship before room selection.
  • Leader reserves; exact room is held immediately.
  • Campaign may require roommate room confirmation within 15 minutes.
  • Payment hold baseline is 72 hours.
  • Shared residents receive separate obligations and pay separately.
  • HL creates separate ROOM and HL obligations.
  • Our app exports SCB Excel; it does not create official QR forms itself.
  • Existing SCB/internal software returns one combined multipage PDF.
  • Payment result is available through an SCB downloadable report.
  • Contracts are printed and physically signed.
  • Renewal and university handoff are part of the operational workflow.

Evidence-quality rule

Do not present provisional values as official policy. Label each item:

  • Confirmed by stakeholder
  • Current product decision
  • Provisional design assumption
  • Need verification

Presentation phrases to avoid

  • “ระบบสร้าง QR เอง”
  • “Ref.1/Ref.2 ไม่ซ้ำต่อคน”
  • “กดจองแล้วได้ห้องทันที”
  • “เราจะแทน SCB หรือ ERP”
  • “ทุกหอใช้ flow เดียวกัน”
