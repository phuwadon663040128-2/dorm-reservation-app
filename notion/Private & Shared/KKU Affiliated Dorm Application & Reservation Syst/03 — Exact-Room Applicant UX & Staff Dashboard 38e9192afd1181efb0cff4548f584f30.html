03 — Exact-Room Applicant UX & Staff Dashboard

Purpose

Last revised: 2026-07-10

This page defines the current UX for exact-room reservation, roommate confirmation, SCB payment documents, annual renewal, printed contracts, and staff operations for หอ 8 หลัง + วรอินเตอร์.

The previous room-type-only and shared-static-QR UX is superseded.

Applicant UX principles

Applicants must always understand:

  • which exact room they are choosing;
  • whether it is shared or whole-room rental;
  • who the roommate is;
  • which holds are active and when they expire;
  • which separate payment actions are required;
  • whether the official SCB QR payment form is ready;
  • whether every roommate payment is complete;
  • whether the room is temporarily held or permanently confirmed;
  • whether contracts and key handover are complete.

KKU SSO is optional. Verified personal-email accounts support the full pilot workflow.

Main applicant flow — shared room

Applicant profiles completed
→ leader sends roommate invitation
→ roommate accepts within 48 hours
→ leader browses building/floor/exact rooms
→ leader selects room and clicks Reserve
→ room held immediately
→ roommate confirms within 15 minutes when campaign setting requires it
→ group enters 72-hour payment hold
→ separate payment obligations created for both residents
→ official SCB QR payment-form pages become available after staff batch processing
→ each resident pays own obligations
→ staff imports/confirms SCB result
→ all required obligations complete
→ staff confirms exact room
→ two contracts generated under one grouped reservation
→ residents print/sign; may sign separately
→ staff records both signed contracts and separate key handover

Main applicant flow — whole-room rental

Applicant selects Whole Room / เหมาห้อง
→ applicant selects exact room
→ room held immediately
→ full-room obligations created per payment action
→ applicant pays official SCB QR forms
→ staff confirms payment and reservation
→ one contract generated with whole-room occupancy/price fields
→ applicant signs physically
→ staff records contract and key handover

Required applicant screens

1. Account entry

  • Sign in with KKU SSO, when available.
  • Create verified personal-email account.
  • Explain that KKU SSO is optional and does not block reservation or contract signing.

2. Campaign landing

Show:

  • dorm group;
  • renewal/new-applicant eligibility;
  • application dates;
  • payment-hold duration;
  • roommate confirmation rule;
  • contract period;
  • key policies and contact.

3. Building / floor / room browser

Required hierarchy:

Dorm group → Building → Floor → Exact room

Each room card/row shows:

  • unique room number;
  • room configuration: normal, air-conditioned, HL, special/corner, etc.;
  • occupancy choices: shared / whole room;
  • room dimensions or plan when available;
  • facilities/equipment;
  • resident price estimate and split payment actions;
  • status: Available / Temporarily Held / Reserved / Unavailable;
  • hold-expiration time when temporarily held.

The MVP may use a structured room list instead of an interactive floor-plan map.

4. Roommate screen

States:

  • No roommate group
  • Invitation sent, expires in 48 hours
  • Invitation received
  • Accepted group
  • Declined/expired
  • Room confirmation required
  • Group ready for payment

Rules:

  • both profiles must be complete;
  • maximum two residents;
  • only one active invitation/group per resident;
  • leader identity is clear;
  • campaign displays whether second room confirmation is required.

5. Exact-room confirmation

When leader clicks Reserve:

  • immediately mark the room held;
  • show 15-minute countdown when roommate confirmation is required;
  • notify roommate in-app and by available notification channel;
  • release immediately on decline/expiry;
  • after success, show the 72-hour payment deadline.

6. Reservation status

Show separate cards:

  • Room & occupancy
  • Roommate group
  • Room hold
  • Payment actions
  • Reservation
  • Contracts
  • Key handover

Never collapse these into one vague status.

Example:

Room: A101 — Temporarily Held until 18:00
Roommate: Confirmed
ROOM payment: Payment Form Ready / Paid / Exception
HL payment: Payment Form Ready / Paid / Exception
Reservation: Pending All Payments / Confirmed
Contracts: 1 of 2 Signed
Key Handover: Not Ready

7. Payment obligations

For each obligation show:

  • resident name;
  • room number (Ref.1);
  • action (Ref.2);
  • amount;
  • bill issue date;
  • payment deadline;
  • official QR payment-form page;
  • current SCB result/status;
  • exception or replacement history.

Applicants do not upload a slip in the normal flow. The official returned SCB payment-form PDF is the payment document.

8. Printed contract

Show:

  • contract preview/download;
  • grouped reservation/roommate context;
  • print instructions;
  • required photo/supporting documents;
  • signing appointment/location when configured;
  • individual signing status;
  • private signed-scan/photo status.

Shared roommates may sign separately, but the group remains incomplete until both contracts are received.

9. Renewal

  • prefill reusable data;
  • highlight changed fields requiring reconfirmation;
  • show current room and roommate;
  • if one roommate does not renew, present invite replacement / whole room / move / release choices before deadline.

Required staff screens

1. Dashboard

Widgets:

  • available exact rooms;
  • held for roommate confirmation;
  • held for payment;
  • holds expiring soon;
  • confirmed rooms;
  • roommate invitations pending/expired;
  • payment obligations ready for export;
  • SCB batches awaiting returned PDF;
  • PDF pages unmatched;
  • payment-result exceptions;
  • contracts ready/printed/signed;
  • key handover pending;
  • university handoff queued/corrected.

2. Room inventory

  • manage dorm/building/floor/exact room;
  • room dimensions/plan;
  • room configuration and equipment;
  • occupancy capability;
  • availability/blocking reason;
  • current hold/reservation;
  • historical configuration versions.

3. Manual reservation

Authorized dorm staff can:

  • select one or two residents;
  • choose exact room and occupancy mode;
  • create final reservation group without resident confirmation;
  • record mandatory reason and audit event.

4. Pricing and obligations

  • show system calculation;
  • flag exception/override;
  • finance role can override current obligation with mandatory reason;
  • permanent pricing-rule change is a separate action;
  • HL generates separate ROOM and HL obligations.

5. SCB export batch

  • filter/select ready obligations;
  • validate bank field limits;
  • preview rows;
  • leave ID blank;
  • export exact .xlsx/SLIPS format;
  • preserve batch and row history;
  • configurable grouping, with current baseline allowing one combined batch.

6. Returned PDF import

  • upload combined multipage PDF;
  • show page thumbnails/text;
  • auto-match using resident-specific text and obligation context;
  • never rely only on room/action or sort order;
  • queue unmatched/ambiguous pages for authorized review;
  • preserve original combined PDF and page mapping.

7. Payment result import

  • upload SCB result report;
  • auto-match transactions;
  • show paid/unpaid/exception results;
  • under/over/duplicate/late payments go to review;
  • manual payment requires transaction/reference plus staff note.

8. Group payment completion

  • show every required obligation for both roommates;
  • use one shared deadline;
  • authorized staff confirms room only when all required payments are complete;
  • if incomplete at expiry, release/cancel under provisional rule.

9. Contract and key handover

  • generate two grouped resident contracts or one whole-room contract;
  • print/reprint with reason;
  • record signed paper and private scan/photo;
  • track group completion;
  • record separate signed key-handover document.

10. University handoff

  • queue confirmed residents until official round opens;
  • export required rows;
  • create correction export without overwriting original submission;
  • show handoff and correction status.

Permission-aware UX

Important permissions:

  • room.manage
  • reservation.manual_create
  • reservation.assign_room
  • payment_obligation.override
  • payment_export.create
  • payment_document.import
  • payment_result.import
  • payment.manual_record
  • payment.confirm
  • contract.generate
  • contract.print
  • contract.receive
  • key_handover.record
  • university_export.create
  • correction_export.create

Manual reservation/payment, amount override, cancellation, roommate replacement, PDF rematch/replacement, contract reprint/correction, and university correction require reason and audit history.

Figma priority

Design first:

  1. Exact-room browser
  1. Room details and occupancy selector
  1. Roommate invitation/acceptance
  1. Leader reservation + 15-minute confirmation countdown
  1. 72-hour payment-hold status
  1. Resident payment-obligation list and SCB PDF page
  1. Staff SCB batch export
  1. Returned multipage PDF import/matching
  1. Payment-result import and exception review
  1. Shared-group confirmation dashboard
  1. Contract and key-handover flow
  1. Renewal with roommate change

Out of scope

  • parcel notification;
  • maintenance workflow;
  • monthly water/electricity implementation, although the future data model should be considered;
  • digital contract signature;
  • direct real-time bank API for MVP.
