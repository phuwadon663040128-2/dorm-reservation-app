02 — Exact-Room Reservation, Roommates & Holds

Purpose

Last revised: 2026-07-10

This page defines the accepted exact-room reservation model for the pilot covering วรเรสซิเดนซ์ / หอ 8 หลัง and หอพักวรอินเตอร์ together.

The previous room-type-quota-first rule is superseded. Exact room, occupancy mode, roommate group, payment obligations, and annual contract are core MVP data.

Core room model

  • Every exact room has a globally unique room number.
  • วรอินเตอร์ uses a building letter plus floor/room number, such as A101.
  • หอ 8 หลัง uses a numeric room format that identifies its building/floor/room.
  • The first-release room browser uses building → floor → exact-room list with availability status and room-size/plan information where available.
  • Maximum normal occupancy is two residents.
  • Supported occupancy modes:
    • Shared — two separate residents;
    • Whole Room / เหมาห้อง — one resident pays the whole-room amount and blocks the second occupant slot.
  • The system should support room configuration changes over time while preserving historical snapshots used by old payments and contracts.

Room statuses

Recommended room inventory statuses:

Unavailable
Available
Invitation Pending, no room held yet
Held for Roommate Confirmation
Held for Payment
Confirmed
Blocked by Staff
Released

A public status may simplify these to Available, Temporarily Held, Reserved, or Unavailable.

Applicant prerequisites

Before joining a roommate group or reserving:

  • applicant account exists;
  • personal email is verified;
  • required profile information is complete;
  • applicant is not in another active roommate group/reservation;
  • applicant is eligible for the campaign.

KKU SSO is optional and is not a reservation gate.

Roommate group model

Group size

  • Maximum two residents.
  • One resident is the reservation leader.
  • One resident may have only one active invitation/group at a time.

Invitation flow

Leader selects a person with a complete profile
→ leader sends roommate invitation
→ invitation valid for 48 hours
→ invited resident accepts or declines
→ accepted pair becomes a roommate group
→ leader may proceed to exact-room selection

The roommate relationship must be accepted before room selection.

Reservation-control setting

Campaign setting:

roommate_room_confirmation_required = true / false

When enabled:

leader selects exact room and clicks Reserve
→ room is held immediately
→ roommate has 15 minutes to confirm the selected room
→ confirmation succeeds: move to payment hold
→ confirmation expires/declines: release room immediately

When disabled:

accepted roommate group
→ leader selects exact room and clicks Reserve
→ room is held immediately
→ no second room confirmation
→ move to payment hold

The leader initiates reservation in both modes.

Payment hold

After the reservation group is ready:

  • exact room is held for 72 hours under the current design baseline;
  • shared-room residents use one group deadline;
  • other applicants can see that the room is temporarily held and when it expires;
  • they cannot reserve the held room;
  • unpaid expiry releases the room exactly once.

If one roommate pays and the other does not:

  • keep the room/group pending until the shared deadline;
  • after expiry, release/cancel the group under the provisional rule;
  • retain the paid obligation and refund/manual-resolution status for staff review.

This policy is marked for later stakeholder review but is the implementation baseline for design.

Shared-room payment rule

  • Each resident receives separate payment obligations.
  • Each resident normally pays their own obligations.
  • Both residents can share Ref.1 and Ref.2 because these are room/action references, not unique identifiers.
  • Permanent confirmation currently requires every required payment action for both residents plus staff confirmation.

Whole-room rental rule

  • One resident occupies the room.
  • Second occupant slot is blocked.
  • One full-room payment obligation is created per payment action.
  • One resident contract is generated.
  • A shared reservation may convert to whole-room rental after one resident has paid, but the system must recalculate and supersede old obligations rather than editing history in place.

Manual staff reservation

Authorized dorm staff may:

  • select exact room;
  • choose shared or whole-room occupancy;
  • attach one or two residents;
  • create the reservation group;
  • create/adjust obligations according to permission;
  • finalize the resident confirmation step.

A staff-created manual reservation is final and does not require resident confirmation. It requires actor, reason, scope, and audit event.

Reservation confirmation

Current provisional rule:

all required payment obligations received
+ authorized staff confirmation
→ reservation Confirmed
→ exact room permanently locked

Physical contract signing occurs after reservation confirmation and does not itself create the reservation.

Renewal flow

Annual renewal is a core workflow.

renewal campaign opens with configurable dates
→ existing profile and room data are prefilled
→ resident confirms/updates changed information
→ roommate pair renews or changes composition
→ exact room is retained/changed according to policy
→ new payment obligations are created
→ payment confirmed
→ new annual contracts generated

If only one roommate renews, give that resident a configured deadline to:

  • invite a replacement;
  • change to whole-room rental;
  • move to another room;
  • release the room.

Reservation leader may change during renewal when both residents confirm.

Roommate replacement

Replacement does not overwrite the old resident.

Required behavior:

  • authorized staff or permitted workflow approves the replacement;
  • old obligations/contracts are superseded;
  • new resident receives new obligations/contracts;
  • deposits and refund status are recorded separately;
  • exact room and group history remain traceable;
  • changes after university handoff produce a correction export.

Concurrency and integrity

  • Exact-room hold creation must be atomic.
  • Two leaders cannot hold the same room simultaneously.
  • Expiry/release is idempotent.
  • Group size cannot exceed two.
  • A resident cannot belong to two active groups.
  • Whole-room occupancy blocks a second resident.
  • Manual and applicant reservation paths use the same transactional domain service.
