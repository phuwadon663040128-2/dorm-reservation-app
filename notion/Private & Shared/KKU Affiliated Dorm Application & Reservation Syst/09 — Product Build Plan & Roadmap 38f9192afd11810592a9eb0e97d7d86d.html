Canonical delivery plan

Last revised: 2026-07-10

This page is the authoritative eight-month delivery plan for the combined หอ 8 หลัง + วรอินเตอร์ pilot.

The accepted MVP now includes exact-room inventory, roommate groups, whole-room rental, SCB Excel export, returned multipage PDF matching, SCB result import, annual renewal, printed contracts, key handover, and university-system handoff.

The following prior assumptions are superseded:

  • one pilot dorm only;
  • room-type quota without exact-room data;
  • exact-room and roommate booking as future-only;
  • one shared static QR and applicant slip upload as the normal payment flow.

Delivery outcome

By the end of Month 8, the team must deliver a reproducible pilot-ready web system proving this complete workflow:

configure exact-room inventory and campaign
→ verified applicant accounts
→ renewal or new application
→ roommate invitation or whole-room choice
→ exact-room selection
→ short confirmation hold
→ 72-hour payment hold
→ resident payment obligations
→ SCB XLSX export
→ returned QR PDF import and page matching
→ SCB result import / manual payment
→ staff confirms exact room
→ printed contracts and signed scans
→ key handover
→ university handoff and correction export

Month 1 decision gate

Confirm or obtain:

  • official building/floor/room inventory source;
  • official room dimensions/plans;
  • room configuration and equipment list;
  • pricing for shared/whole-room and HL;
  • fee-exempt occupant policy;
  • semester-installment Ref.2 format;
  • original SCB blank Excel template;
  • returned PDF sample for roommates;
  • SCB payment-result report sample;
  • final reservation-confirmation wording;
  • renewal dates and one-paid/one-unpaid policy;
  • contract template, copies, attachments, retention, and key-handover checklist;
  • university handoff columns/correction format;
  • exact staff role names and scopes.

Unconfirmed values remain clearly labelled Provisional.

Provisional development rules

  1. Exact room is the inventory source of truth.
  1. Roommate invitation is accepted before room selection.
  1. Leader reservation creates an immediate room hold.
  1. Required roommate room confirmation expires after 15 minutes.
  1. Group payment hold lasts 72 hours.
  1. Shared residents have separate obligations and one group deadline.
  1. HL creates separate ROOM and HL obligations.
  1. All required obligations plus authorized staff confirmation permanently confirms the room.
  1. One paid / one unpaid group remains pending until deadline, then releases under provisional rule.
  1. Manual reservation/payment, override, cancellation, replacement, rematch, reprint, and correction require reason and audit.
  1. Repeated holds, releases, imports, and confirmations are idempotent.

Lifecycle models

Room/hold lifecycle

Available
→ Held for Roommate Confirmation
→ Held for Payment
→ Confirmed / Released / Expired / Cancelled

Roommate-group lifecycle

Invitation Pending
→ Accepted
→ Room Confirmation Pending when required
→ Ready for Payment
→ Confirmed / Cancelled / Replaced

Payment-document lifecycle

Not Generated
→ Ready for Export
→ Exported
→ Awaiting Returned PDF
→ Payment Form Ready
→ Superseded / Cancelled

Payment-result lifecycle

Awaiting Payment
→ Result Imported / Manual Recorded
→ Paid / Unpaid / Exception
→ Confirmed / Refund Status / Cancelled

Contract lifecycle

Not Generated
→ Ready to Generate
→ Ready to Print
→ Printed
→ Signed and Received / Correction Required / Reprinted / Cancelled

Key-handover lifecycle

Not Ready
→ Ready
→ Signed and Handed Over
→ Corrected / Cancelled

  • exact-room source identified;
  • SCB template/PDF/result samples obtained or explicitly tracked;
  • contract and handoff unknowns assigned owners.

MS2

  • room, group, hold, payment, contract, and handoff states approved;
  • wireframes cover shared and whole-room flows;
  • permissions and audit rules approved;
  • traceability matrix complete.

MS3

  • clean environment starts via Docker Compose;
  • auth and private file storage work;
  • exact-room fixture imports;
  • sample XLSX/PDF/result artifacts process end-to-end.

MS4

  • roommate and whole-room applicants can reserve exact rooms safely;
  • 15-minute and 72-hour holds work;
  • concurrent reservation cannot double-book;
  • renewal and manual reservation work.

MS5

  • correct obligations generated;
  • SCB workbook matches template;
  • PDF pages map without room/action uniqueness assumption;
  • result import/manual payment works;
  • exceptions and final confirmation work.

MS6

  • contracts, scans, key handover, reports, audit, and university correction workflow complete.

MS7

  • UAT accepted;
  • concurrency, authorization, files, import idempotency, privacy, backup/restore tests pass.

MS8

  • tagged release;
  • deployment package;
  • handover docs;
  • final demo;
  • expansion backlog.

Required high-risk tests

  • two leaders reserve same exact room concurrently;
  • roommate confirmation expires at 15 minutes;
  • payment hold expires at 72 hours;
  • one roommate paid / one unpaid;
  • shared to whole-room conversion after payment;
  • repeated XLSX generation/download;
  • combined PDF reordered with duplicate room/action references;
  • ambiguous PDF page manual rematch;
  • repeated SCB result import;
  • duplicate/under/over/late payment;
  • repeated final confirmation;
  • roommate replacement after university handoff;
  • contract reprint/correction and private scan access;
  • restore database and files together.
