01 — PRD: Affiliated Dorm Application & Reservation System

Product requirements baseline

Last revised: 2026-07-10

This is the current source-of-truth PRD for the KKU Affiliated Dorm Application & Reservation System.

The accepted pilot covers วรเรสซิเดนซ์ / หอ 8 หลัง and หอพักวรอินเตอร์ together. The previous room-type-quota-first and shared-static-QR assumptions are superseded.

Product name

English: KKU Affiliated Dorm Application & Reservation System

Thai: ระบบรับสมัครและจองหอพักในกำกับ มข. พร้อมจัดการห้องจริง การชำระเงิน และสัญญา

Product direction

The product supports the real early-reservation workflow of the pilot dorms:

  • applicants can start before receiving a KKU account;
  • residents select an exact room;
  • shared rooms use roommate invitation and grouped reservation;
  • one resident may rent the whole room;
  • exact-room holds protect against concurrent booking;
  • the system creates resident payment obligations;
  • finance exports the required SCB Bill Payment Excel file;
  • the existing SCB/internal application returns official QR payment-form PDFs and a payment-result report;
  • staff confirms payment and the exact room;
  • the system generates annual paper contracts for physical signing;
  • confirmed records queue for later university-system handoff.

Pilot scope

Included dorm groups

  • วรเรสซิเดนซ์ / หอ 8 หลัง
  • หอพักวรอินเตอร์

They are treated as one pilot programme with configurable dorm/building/payment settings.

Exact-room inventory

  • Room numbers are globally unique.
  • วรอินเตอร์ room numbers encode building letter plus floor/room, such as A101.
  • หอ 8 หลัง uses numeric room identifiers that encode building/floor/room.
  • Applicant room selection uses building → floor → exact room.
  • Room-size/plan information should be shown when reliable source data is available.

Occupancy modes

  • Shared — two separate residents, two contracts, separate resident obligations.
  • Whole Room / เหมาห้อง — one resident, full-room price, one contract.
  • Maximum normal occupancy: two residents.

Applicant identity

  • One internal applicant/user record is permanent.
  • Applicants without a KKU account register using verified personal email and password.
  • KKU SSO may be used initially or linked later.
  • KKU SSO is optional and does not gate reservation, payment, contract signing, or key handover.
  • Anonymous applications are not allowed.
  • Optional linking must not create duplicate applicant, reservation, payment, or contract records.

Main problem statement

Current operations duplicate work across paper applications, Excel, bank/payment software, signed contracts, and later university systems. Exact room configuration, roommate composition, whole-room pricing, HL split billing, early payment timing, resident changes, and annual renewals make a simple room-type quota system insufficient.

The product must reduce duplicate entry without removing the controls that staff use to prevent false reservations and late cancellations.

Product goals

  1. Provide current exact-room availability for the pilot dorms.
  1. Support shared-room and whole-room reservation.
  1. Support roommate invitation, confirmation, and grouped status.
  1. Hold exact rooms safely during confirmation and payment.
  1. Calculate resident-specific payment obligations.
  1. Support separate ROOM and HL billing.
  1. Export the bank-required SCB Excel template.
  1. Import and match returned QR payment-form PDF pages.
  1. Import the SCB payment-result report and manage exceptions.
  1. Confirm reservations only after required payments and staff confirmation.
  1. Reuse resident data for annual renewal.
  1. Generate printable annual contracts and track signed copies.
  1. Record separate key handover.
  1. Queue confirmed residents for university-system handoff and corrections.
  1. Preserve role-based access and audit history.

Non-goals for the MVP

  • digital/electronic contract signature;
  • direct real-time bank API;
  • automated refund execution;
  • parcel notification;
  • maintenance workflow;
  • monthly water/electricity billing implementation;
  • central-dorm lottery/fairness allocation;
  • simultaneous launch for every affiliated dorm;
  • native mobile application.

The data model should remain extensible for monthly utilities later.

User roles

Applicant / resident

Can:

  • register before receiving a KKU account;
  • browse exact rooms;
  • create/accept roommate invitation;
  • reserve as leader;
  • confirm selected room when required;
  • choose shared or whole-room occupancy;
  • view room/payment/contract/key status;
  • download official SCB QR payment-form pages;
  • print their contract;
  • upload signed-contract scan/photo when allowed or follow staff instructions;
  • renew and update profile;
  • request allowed resident/room changes.

Reservation leader

  • creates roommate invitation;
  • initiates exact-room reservation;
  • remains the primary initiator under both control modes;
  • does not own the roommate’s separate payment or contract.

Authorized dorm staff

Can, within scope:

  • manage exact rooms;
  • block/unblock rooms;
  • create manual reservations;
  • assign room and occupancy mode;
  • review applicant/group status;
  • manage renewal/replacement;
  • confirm room after payment when permitted;
  • manage contracts and key handover when separately authorized.

Finance/payment staff

Can, within scope:

  • review pricing exceptions;
  • override current obligation with reason;
  • create SCB export batch;
  • download XLSX;
  • import returned combined PDF;
  • review page mapping;
  • import SCB result report;
  • record manual payment with bank reference and note;
  • resolve payment exceptions;
  • confirm payment completion when authorized;
  • track refund status.

Contract staff

Can, within scope:

  • manage approved templates;
  • generate/preview contracts;
  • print/reprint with reason;
  • record signed receipt and private scan/photo;
  • correct/cancel while preserving history;
  • record key handover.

Dorm service division admin / super admin

Can:

  • manage roles and scopes;
  • view cross-dorm reports;
  • manage system configuration;
  • review audit logs;
  • manage university handoff and corrections.

Core business rules

Roommate prerequisites

  • Both residents must have complete profiles.
  • Maximum group size is two.
  • One active invitation/group per resident.
  • Invitation valid for 48 hours.
  • Roommate accepts relationship before exact-room selection.

Room reservation

  • Leader selects exact room and clicks Reserve.
  • Room hold is created immediately.
  • Campaign may require roommate confirmation of the selected room.
  • Required confirmation deadline: 15 minutes.
  • Failure/decline releases room immediately.
  • Staff manual reservation is final and does not require resident confirmation.

Payment hold

  • After group readiness, exact room is held for 72 hours under current baseline.
  • Shared residents use one group deadline.
  • Other candidates see Temporarily Held and expiry time.
  • They cannot reserve the room.

Shared room

  • Separate payment obligations for each resident.
  • Each resident normally pays their own obligations.
  • Both residents’ required obligations must be complete.
  • If one pays and one does not, keep group until shared deadline, then release/cancel under provisional rule and send paid amount to manual/refund resolution.

Whole-room rental

  • One resident.
  • Full-room price.
  • One obligation per payment action.
  • One contract.
  • Second occupancy slot blocked.

Shared-to-whole conversion

  • Allowed after one roommate has paid.
  • Recalculate price.
  • Supersede old obligations/contracts.
  • Preserve original history.

Payment actions and pricing

ROOM

Room-fee action. Current Ref.2 example: ROOM2569.

HL

Happy Living / converted-air-room service action.

HL rooms always create two obligations:

  • ROOM;
  • HL.

Pricing may depend on:

  • exact room;
  • room configuration;
  • occupancy mode;
  • applicant type;
  • academic year/payment cycle;
  • action.

System calculates normal amounts. Finance reviews only exceptions/overrides.

One-off override:

  • current obligation/export row only;
  • finance permission;
  • mandatory reason and audit event.

Permanent price change:

  • separate versioned pricing-rule action.

SCB Excel export requirements

Output:

  • .xlsx;
  • worksheet named exactly SLIPS;
  • bank-required column order.

Current field mapping:

  • ID — blank;
  • Payer Name * — payment description/title;
  • Ref.1 * — exact room number;
  • Ref.2 — action + Buddhist academic year;
  • Amount * — numeric amount;
  • Payment Date * — bill issue date;
  • Email Address — populated because internal software requires it;
  • Alert Message — DEFAULT;
  • Remark — optional/configurable.

One row equals one resident × one action.

Batch grouping is configurable; current baseline permits all rows in one batch when supported.

The five-baht SCB fee is absorbed by dorm/company under the current assumption.

Returned PDF requirements

  • SCB/internal software returns one combined multipage PDF.
  • Store original PDF privately.
  • Extract/index each page.
  • Match page to resident obligation using resident-specific page text and context.
  • Do not identify by Ref.1 + Ref.2 alone.
  • Do not depend on assumed descending room-number order.
  • Ambiguous pages require authorized manual mapping with reason.
  • Changed bill is cancelled/superseded and replaced; old version remains.

Payment result requirements

  • Import SCB downloadable transaction/result report.
  • Match result rows to obligations.
  • Support manual payment with bank transaction/reference plus staff note.
  • Underpayment, overpayment, duplicate, late, post-expiry, and unmatched results enter staff review.
  • Refund status is recorded; execution occurs outside app.

Reservation confirmation

Current provisional source of truth:

all required resident payment obligations received
+ authorized staff confirmation
→ exact room permanently Confirmed

Contract signing follows reservation confirmation and is tracked separately.

Renewal requirements

  • Configurable renewal campaign dates.
  • Prefill prior reusable data.
  • Resident confirms/edits changes.
  • Same-room and roommate context displayed.
  • One remaining roommate gets a deadline to invite replacement, buy out, move, or release.
  • Leader may change with both residents’ confirmation.
  • Replacement supersedes old obligations/contracts and preserves history.

Contract requirements

Shared room

  • Two separate resident contracts.
  • Same grouped reservation and room.
  • Available after both residents complete required payments.
  • Residents may sign separately.
  • Group incomplete until both contracts received.

Whole room

  • Same template with occupancy/price values changed.
  • One resident contract.

Common requirements

  • Applicant or staff may print.
  • Signed scan/photo required and stored privately.
  • Physical photo/supporting paper may be attached to printed contract when required.
  • Reprint/correction/cancellation preserves prior versions and requires reason.

Key handover

  • Separate signed record linked to contract.
  • Only after required payment, contract, and supporting-document checks.
  • Exact checklist remains stakeholder data.

University-system handoff

  • Queue confirmed residents until official round opens.
  • Export approved records.
  • Preserve batch and row history.
  • Later changes create separate correction record/export.
  • Never overwrite original submitted record.

Status models

Room hold / reservation

Available
→ Held for Roommate Confirmation
→ Held for Payment
→ Confirmed / Released / Expired / Cancelled

Roommate group

Invitation Pending
→ Accepted
→ Room Confirmation Pending when required
→ Ready for Payment
→ Confirmed / Cancelled / Replaced

Payment document

Not Generated
→ Ready for Export
→ Exported
→ Awaiting Returned PDF
→ Payment Form Ready
→ Superseded / Cancelled

Payment result

Awaiting Payment
→ Result Imported / Manual Recorded
→ Paid / Unpaid / Exception
→ Confirmed / Refund Status / Cancelled

Contract

Not Generated
→ Ready to Generate
→ Ready to Print
→ Printed
→ Signed and Received / Correction Required / Reprinted / Cancelled

Key handover

Not Ready
→ Ready
→ Signed and Handed Over
→ Corrected / Cancelled
