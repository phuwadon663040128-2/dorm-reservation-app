Why the product changed

Original concept

First live-product pivot

Current validated direction

Stakeholder meetings and operational artifacts showed that the pilot needs:

  • exact-room inventory;
  • shared/whole-room occupancy;
  • roommate groups;
  • early room holds;
  • resident-specific payment obligations;
  • ROOM and HL split billing;
  • SCB Excel export;
  • returned QR payment PDFs;
  • SCB payment-result import;
  • annual renewal;
  • printed contracts and key handover;
  • later university-system handoff.

Key operational facts

Exact rooms

  • Room numbers are globally unique.
  • วรอินเตอร์ includes a building letter in room number.
  • หอ 8 หลัง uses a numeric structure identifying building/floor/room.
  • Applicants can choose the exact room.

Occupancy

  • Maximum two residents.
  • Shared rooms have two individual residents/contracts/obligations.
  • Whole-room rental lets one resident occupy/pay for the whole room.

Roommate reservation

  • Both profiles must exist and be complete.
  • Leader sends an invitation.
  • Invitation expires after 48 hours.
  • Roommate accepts relationship before room selection.
  • Leader reserves exact room; hold begins immediately.
  • Campaign may require roommate confirmation within 15 minutes.
  • Group then receives a 72-hour payment hold.

Payment

  • Each shared resident normally pays their own obligations.
  • Whole-room resident pays full-room obligations.
  • HL room creates separate ROOM and HL actions.
  • Our app exports bank-required SLIPS Excel.
  • Existing SCB/internal software generates one combined multipage QR PDF.
  • Our app imports/matches PDF pages and payment-result report.
  • Five-baht SCB fee is absorbed by dorm/company under current baseline.

Confirmation

Provisional rule:

all required payments received
+ authorized staff confirmation
→ exact room confirmed

Contracts

  • Annual paper contracts remain.
  • Shared room: two contracts under one reservation group.
  • Whole room: one contract.
  • Applicant or staff may print.
  • Signed scan/photo stored privately.
  • Key handover is separate signed record.

Renewal and changes

  • Renewal is core.
  • Previous data is prefilled.
  • One remaining roommate may invite replacement, buy out, move, or release.
  • Replacement supersedes old obligations/contracts and preserves history.
  • Changes after university submission create correction exports.

Product thesis

The pilot dorms need a single operational system that knows the exact room, actual residents, roommate group, occupancy mode, resident payment obligations, official SCB payment documents/results, annual contracts, and later university handoff—without forcing staff to retype the same data across paper and spreadsheets.

Current MVP in one sentence

A web system where applicants or existing residents form a roommate group or choose whole-room rental, reserve an exact room, complete SCB-generated payment obligations, receive confirmation, sign printed annual contracts, and move into an auditable university handoff process.

Stakeholder positioning

We are not replacing SCB, Payment Hub, ERP, or university accounting. We are building the exact-room and resident workflow before those systems: application, roommate grouping, holds, pricing, batch preparation, document matching, payment status, contracts, and clean handoff.

Decision history

  • Fairness/allocation-first: superseded.
  • Room-type quota only: superseded for pilot.
  • Shared static QR/slip-upload normal flow: superseded.
  • KKU SSO as contract gate: rejected.
  • Exact rooms, SCB bulk workflow, and paper contracts: accepted.
