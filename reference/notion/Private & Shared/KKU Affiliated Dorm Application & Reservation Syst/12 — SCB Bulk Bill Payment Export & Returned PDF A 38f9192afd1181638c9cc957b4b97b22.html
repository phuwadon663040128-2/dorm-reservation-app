12 — SCB Bulk Bill Payment Export & Returned PDF Architecture

Decision record

Decision date: 2026-07-10

Status: Accepted current MVP direction

Supersedes: one shared static QR, applicant slip upload as the normal path, and direct bank/payment-provider API assumptions

The pilot payment flow uses the existing SCB Bill Payment bulk process. Our application prepares exact-room and resident payment obligations, exports the bank-required Excel workbook, staff uploads it to the existing internal SCB application, and that application returns official QR payment-form PDFs. Payment results are then imported from the SCB downloadable result/transaction report or confirmed manually by authorized finance staff.

Pilot scope

  • Pilot dorm groups: วรเรสซิเดนซ์ / หอ 8 หลัง and หอพักวรอินเตอร์ together.
  • Exact room inventory is authoritative; room numbers are globally unique and encode the building/floor context.
  • Maximum normal occupancy is two residents per room.
  • Occupancy modes: shared room or whole-room rental (เหมาห้อง).
  • A normal shared room creates separate resident payment obligations.
  • A whole-room rental creates one resident obligation for the full-room amount per payment action.
  • HL rooms create separate ROOM and HL payment obligations.
  • The five-baht SCB Bill Payment fee is absorbed by the dorm/company under the current design assumption.

Reservation and payment holds

Roommate confirmation hold

  • The leader selects the exact room and clicks Reserve.
  • The room is held immediately.
  • When campaign setting roommate_room_confirmation_required is enabled, the roommate has 15 minutes to confirm the selected room.
  • If confirmation does not occur in time, the room is released immediately.
  • Staff-created manual reservations are final and do not require resident confirmation.

Payment hold

  • After the reservation group is ready, the exact room is held for 72 hours under the current design baseline.
  • Shared-room residents use one room-group deadline.
  • Other applicants may see that the room is temporarily held and when the hold expires, but they cannot reserve it.
  • Permanent confirmation currently requires all required payment actions to be received and an authorized staff confirmation.
  • If one roommate pays and the other does not, the room remains held until the shared deadline, then the group is released/cancelled under the provisional rule.

Payment actions

ROOM

Official room-fee obligation. Ref.2 is currently expected to combine payment-action code and Buddhist academic year, for example ROOM2569.

HL

Happy Living / converted-air-room service obligation. HL rooms always create a separate HL obligation in addition to ROOM. Equipment/services included in HL remain configurable.

Future actions

Additional codes and semester-installment encoding remain configuration/stakeholder data. Do not hard-code only ROOM and HL into the domain model.

SCB Excel export contract

The workbook must use .xlsx, preserve the required worksheet name SLIPS, and keep the bank-required column order.

Current field mapping:

  • ID — must be left blank.
  • Payer Name * — payment description/title, not assumed to be the resident name.
  • Ref.1 * — exact room number; repeated for roommates in the same room.
  • Ref.2 — payment action code plus academic year, such as ROOM2569 or HL2569.
  • Amount * — numeric resident obligation amount.
  • Payment Date * — bill issue date under the current interpretation.
  • Email Address — required by the internal application; precise operational use remains unconfirmed.
  • Alert Message — DEFAULT.
  • Remark — optional/configurable note subject to the template limit.

One export row represents:

one resident × one payment action

Example for two roommates in an HL room:

Resident A | A101 | ROOM2569 | amount A-room
Resident A | A101 | HL2569   | amount A-HL
Resident B | A101 | ROOM2569 | amount B-room
Resident B | A101 | HL2569   | amount B-HL

Batch grouping is configurable. The current baseline allows all rows in one batch when the internal application accepts them.

Amount calculation and override

  • The system calculates ordinary amounts from room, occupancy mode, applicant type, academic year, payment action, and configured pricing rules.
  • Finance/payment administrators review only exceptions and overrides rather than every normal row.
  • A payment-obligation override affects only the current obligation/export row.
  • Every override requires a reason and audit event.
  • Permanent pricing changes are a separate configuration action and must not occur silently through a one-off override.

Returned PDF handling

The current SCB output is one combined multipage PDF.

Required behavior:

  • Upload the returned PDF to private storage.
  • Preserve the original batch file and checksum.
  • Split or index pages without losing the original combined file.
  • Match each page to the correct resident payment obligation using resident-specific text printed on the page.
  • Do not rely on page order or assumed descending Ref.1, because roommates can share identical Ref.1 and Ref.2.
  • Record export row number, PDF page number, matching result, reviewer, and confidence/manual correction.
  • A changed bill is cancelled/superseded and replaced with a new version; old versions remain in history.

Payment-result import and confirmation

Normal source:

  • SCB downloadable transaction/result report.

Fallback/manual source:

  • Authorized finance staff records bank transaction/reference plus staff note.

Payment exceptions such as underpayment, overpayment, duplicate payment, late payment, and payment after hold expiry enter staff review. Refund status is tracked in our application, but refund execution remains outside the MVP and may differ between ROOM and HL recipients.

Core data model

Recommended entities:

  • payment_actions
  • pricing_rules
  • payment_obligations
  • payment_export_batches
  • payment_export_rows
  • returned_payment_documents
  • returned_payment_document_pages
  • payment_result_imports
  • payment_transactions
  • payment_adjustments
  • payment_status_events

Key constraints:

  • Every obligation belongs to one resident, reservation group, exact room, academic year/payment cycle, and payment action.
  • Ref.1 + Ref.2 is not unique across residents.
  • Internal obligation ID is the source of truth.
  • Export row order is metadata, not identity.
  • One transaction/result row must not satisfy multiple obligations unless an explicitly approved allocation rule exists.
  • Confirmation and room-lock updates must be transaction-safe and idempotent.

Remaining decisions

  • Whether ROOM and HL can always coexist in one SCB upload across receiving accounts.
  • Resident-specific field printed on returned PDF pages.
  • Actual PDF sort order and whether SCB returns a manifest.
  • Exact columns and identifiers in the SCB payment-result report.
  • Final stakeholder confirmation that all required payments plus staff confirmation means “ได้ห้องแล้ว”.
