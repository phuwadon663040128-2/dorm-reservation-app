15 — Mock SSO, Exact-Room, SCB Export/PDF & Payment Fixtures

Purpose

Last revised: 2026-07-10

This page defines development fixtures for the current pilot direction: verified applicant accounts, optional Mock KKU SSO, exact-room reservation, roommate confirmation, SCB bulk Excel export, returned multipage PDF matching, SCB payment-result import, printed contracts, and key handover.

The payment fixture must model the existing operational file exchange. It must not pretend that our app is the bank or that a shared static QR is the official payment method.

Docker services

mock-kku-sso    Optional local KKU-style OIDC provider

Internal email/password authentication, SCB batch generation, PDF fixtures, payment-result fixtures, and contract fixtures live inside the application development/test environment.

Recommended configuration:

KKU_SSO_ISSUER=http://mock-kku-sso:8088/mock/kku-sso
KKU_SSO_CLIENT_ID=kku-dorm-dev
KKU_SSO_CLIENT_SECRET=<dev-only mock secret>
SCB_SLIPS_TEMPLATE_PATH=/fixtures/scb/slips-template.xlsx
SCB_RETURNED_PDF_PATH=/fixtures/scb/returned-payment-forms.pdf
SCB_PAYMENT_RESULT_PATH=/fixtures/scb/payment-result.xlsx

Do not commit production credentials, real resident data, real bank references, or real contracts.

Applicant and optional identity fixtures

  • verified personal-email applicant without KKU account;
  • applicant using Mock KKU SSO initially;
  • applicant optionally linking Mock KKU SSO later;
  • unverified personal email blocked from application submission;
  • duplicate KKU provider subject blocked from a second user;
  • controlled profile mismatch sent to optional identity-link review.

KKU link status must not gate application, payment, reservation, contract generation, physical signing, or key handover.

Exact-room fixtures

Pilot fixture inventory must include both dorm groups.

Examples:

  • วรอินเตอร์ room A101 where A identifies the building and 101 identifies floor/room;
  • หอ 8 หลัง room number whose numeric format identifies its building/floor/room;
  • normal shared room;
  • normal whole-room rental;
  • HL shared room;
  • HL whole-room rental;
  • special/corner room;
  • temporarily held room;
  • permanently confirmed room;
  • unavailable/maintenance-disabled room, although maintenance workflow itself is outside the MVP.

Room numbers must be globally unique in the fixture set.

Roommate and hold fixtures

Required scenarios:

  • both profiles complete;
  • leader sends invitation;
  • invitation expires after 48 hours;
  • only one active invitation/group per resident;
  • roommate accepts before room selection;
  • leader selects an exact room and clicks Reserve;
  • room is held immediately;
  • campaign requires second room confirmation;
  • roommate confirms within 15 minutes;
  • roommate fails to confirm and the room releases immediately;
  • campaign does not require second confirmation;
  • staff creates a manual reservation and no resident confirmation is required;
  • payment hold lasts 72 hours after the group is ready;
  • other applicants see Temporarily Held and the expiry time;
  • maximum group size is two residents.

Pricing fixtures

  • system-calculated ordinary amount;
  • shared-room amount per resident;
  • whole-room amount for one resident;
  • HL ROOM amount;
  • HL service-company HL amount;
  • first-year full academic-year amount;
  • senior installment amount;
  • one-off finance override with reason;
  • permanent pricing-rule change as a separate action;
  • fee-exempt occupant placeholder marked unresolved.

SCB Excel fixture

The generated workbook must contain worksheet SLIPS and the bank-required columns in order:

Current fixture rules:

  • ID is blank;
  • Payer Name contains a payment description/title;
  • Ref.1 is the exact room number;
  • Ref.2 is the payment action plus Buddhist academic year;
  • Amount is numeric;
  • Payment Date is the bill issue date;
  • Alert Message is DEFAULT;
  • one row equals one resident × one payment action;
  • the five-baht bank fee is not added to the resident amount under the current assumption.

Returned multipage PDF fixtures

Create one combined PDF with:

  • one page per exported row;
  • repeated Ref.1 and Ref.2 for roommates;
  • resident-specific text on each page;
  • output pages reordered from Excel order;
  • a probable descending room-number arrangement;
  • one unmatched page;
  • one duplicate candidate;
  • one replacement/superseded bill.

Required matching tests:

  • match by resident-specific text and obligation context;
  • never match only by Ref.1 + Ref.2;
  • never rely only on page order;
  • allow authorized manual correction;
  • preserve original PDF, checksum, page number, extracted text, and final mapping decision.

SCB payment-result fixtures

Include a fictional downloadable result report with:

  • successful payment;
  • unpaid obligation;
  • underpayment;
  • overpayment;
  • duplicate transaction;
  • late payment;
  • payment received after hold expiry;
  • ROOM paid but HL unpaid;
  • both roommates fully paid;
  • one roommate paid and the other unpaid;
  • result row that cannot be matched automatically.

Manual payment fixture requires:

  • bank transaction/reference;
  • authorized staff user;
  • mandatory staff note;
  • audit event.

Reservation confirmation fixtures

Provisional rule:

all required obligations received
+ authorized staff confirmation
→ exact room permanently confirmed

Test that:

  • a 72-hour unpaid hold expires exactly once;
  • one paid roommate does not permanently confirm the group;
  • conversion from shared room to whole-room rental recalculates and supersedes obligations;
  • repeated confirmation requests are idempotent;
  • concurrent staff actions cannot confirm/release the same room twice.

Contract and key-handover fixtures

  • two separate resident contracts under one grouped shared-room reservation;
  • same room, contract period, and reservation-group identifier;
  • contracts available after both residents complete required payments;
  • residents may sign separately while group status remains incomplete;
  • whole-room contract uses the same template with occupancy/price values changed;
  • applicant-printed and staff-printed variants;
  • required private scan/photo of the signed contract;
  • corrected/reprinted contract preserving prior versions;
  • separate signed key-handover record.

University-system handoff fixtures

  • confirmed residents waiting for the university round;
  • exported handoff batch;
  • original submitted row;
  • later roommate/amount correction;
  • separate correction export preserving the original history.

Required tests

  • Verified personal-email registration creates one internal user and applicant profile.
  • Exact room can be selected only when available.
  • Reservation leader cannot exceed a two-person group.
  • Roommate relationship must be accepted before room selection.
  • Leader reservation creates the immediate room hold.
  • Required second confirmation expires after 15 minutes and releases the room.
  • Staff manual reservation is final and audited.
  • Shared room creates separate resident obligations.
  • Whole-room rental creates one resident full-room obligation per action.
  • HL creates separate ROOM and HL obligations.
  • Amount override requires finance permission, reason, and audit event.
  • XLSX export keeps exact sheet/column contract and blank ID cells.
  • Returned PDF pages match resident obligations without relying on room/action references alone.
  • SCB result import updates the correct obligations and sends exceptions to review.
  • All required payments plus staff confirmation permanently confirm the room.
  • Refund execution stays external while refund status/history is retained.
  • Contract files and scans remain private.
  • Manual reservation/payment, override, cancellation, replacement, reprint, and correction are audited.

Removed from the MVP fixture scope

  • shared static QR configuration;
  • applicant slip upload as the normal payment path;
  • UniPay OAuth/debt/callback endpoints;
  • direct bank API callbacks;
  • exact-room inventory as future-only;
  • roommate/group booking as future-only;
  • monthly water/electricity implementation;
  • parcel and maintenance workflows.
