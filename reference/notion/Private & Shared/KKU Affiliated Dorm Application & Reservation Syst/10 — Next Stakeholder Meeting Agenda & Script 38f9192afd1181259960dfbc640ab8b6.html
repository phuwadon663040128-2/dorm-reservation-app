10 — Next Stakeholder Meeting Agenda & Script

Purpose

Last revised: 2026-07-10

Use this page for the next stakeholder review with พี่อ้อม, พี่แพท/หอ 8 หลัง–วรอินเตอร์ staff, อาจารย์กานดา, finance/payment staff, and the university-system owner.

The next meeting is no longer a vague product pitch. It should validate the exact-room, roommate, SCB bulk payment, contract, and handoff design before Figma/design freeze.

Confirmed direction to present

  • Pilot covers หอ 8 หลัง and วรอินเตอร์ together.
  • Applicants may reserve before receiving KKU accounts.
  • Exact-room selection is core.
  • Maximum two residents per room.
  • Shared room or whole-room rental is supported.
  • Both roommate profiles must be complete.
  • Leader invites roommate; invitation lasts 48 hours.
  • Roommate accepts before room selection.
  • Leader reserves exact room; room is held immediately.
  • Campaign may require second room confirmation within 15 minutes.
  • Payment hold baseline is 72 hours.
  • Shared residents receive separate obligations and pay their own.
  • HL generates separate ROOM and HL obligations.
  • Our app exports the SCB SLIPS Excel file.
  • Existing SCB/internal software generates one combined multipage QR payment PDF.
  • Our app imports/matches the PDF and SCB result report.
  • Full required payment plus staff confirmation is the provisional room-confirmation rule.
  • Contracts are printed and signed physically.
  • Renewal, signed scans, key handover, and later university handoff are core.
