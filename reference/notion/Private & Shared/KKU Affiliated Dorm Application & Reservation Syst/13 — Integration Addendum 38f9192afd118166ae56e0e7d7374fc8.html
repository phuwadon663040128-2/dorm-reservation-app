13 — Integration Addendum

Purpose

This addendum records the current external-system boundary for the KKU affiliated dorm pilot.

Current integration decision

Decision date: 2026-07-10

The pilot does not generate its own official QR payment form and does not call a bank API in real time. It integrates operationally with the existing SCB Bill Payment bulk workflow:

our app creates resident payment obligations
→ our app exports the required XLSX/SLIPS batch
→ authorized staff uploads the batch to the existing SCB/internal application
→ that application returns a combined multipage PDF containing official QR payment forms
→ staff imports the PDF into our app
→ our app matches pages to resident obligations
→ residents pay using the returned QR forms
→ staff imports the SCB payment-result report or records a manual bank reference

Canonical payment design: 12 — SCB Bulk Bill Payment Export & Returned PDF Architecture

Pilot dorm boundary

The accepted pilot covers วรเรสซิเดนซ์ / หอ 8 หลัง and หอพักวรอินเตอร์ together. The product uses exact-room inventory because the room number, occupancy mode, room configuration, and resident-specific amount drive the payment and contract workflow.

Payment-system ownership

Our application owns

  • applicant and resident profiles;
  • roommate grouping and reservation leader;
  • exact-room selection and holds;
  • shared versus whole-room occupancy mode;
  • pricing rules and one-off amount overrides;
  • resident payment obligations;
  • SCB Excel batch generation;
  • returned PDF storage and page matching;
  • imported/manual payment status;
  • reservation confirmation status;
  • printed-contract and key-handover status;
  • university-system handoff queue and correction history.

Existing SCB/internal application owns

  • acceptance of the bank-required Excel template;
  • generation of the official QR payment-form PDF;
  • bank-facing Bill Payment processing;
  • downloadable transaction/result report.

University systems continue to own

  • official university accounting/ERP/Payment Hub records;
  • later payment rounds and official receipt/accounting processes where applicable.

Our app queues confirmed resident records and exports them when the university round opens. It does not overwrite the university system of record.

Reference semantics

  • Ref.1 = globally unique exact room number.
  • Ref.2 = payment action plus Buddhist academic year, for example ROOM2569 or HL2569.
  • Roommates may share the same Ref.1 and Ref.2; these references are grouping/processing fields, not unique resident identifiers.
  • Internal payment-obligation IDs remain authoritative.

HL split-billing boundary

An HL room creates two obligations:

  • ROOM for the university room-fee portion;
  • HL for the Happy Living/service-company portion.

The student-facing room may be presented as an air-conditioned room, while the backend retains the accounting distinction and each recipient/action separately.

Printed-contract boundary

KKU SSO is optional. It does not gate reservation, payment, contract generation, physical signing, or key handover.

After payment confirmation:

  • two roommates receive separate contracts under one grouped reservation;
  • a whole-room renter receives one contract using the same template with whole-room occupancy and pricing values;
  • applicants or staff may print;
  • signed contracts are scanned/photographed and stored privately;
  • key handover is a separate signed record linked to the contract.

Superseded assumptions

The following are no longer the accepted MVP direction:

  • one shared static QR displayed directly by our app;
  • applicant slip upload as the normal payment path;
  • room-type quota without exact-room inventory;
  • exact-room selection and roommate booking as future-only features;
  • direct UniPay/bank API integration as an MVP dependency.

Future integration options

After the bulk workflow is stable and official access is approved, the project may investigate:

  • direct import/export API with the university system;
  • SCB result-report automation;
  • official payment callbacks;
  • automated reconciliation;
  • monthly water/electricity billing integration.

These future options must not block the pilot.
