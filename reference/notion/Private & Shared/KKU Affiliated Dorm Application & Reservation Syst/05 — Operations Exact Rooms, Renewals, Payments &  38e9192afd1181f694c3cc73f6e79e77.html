05 — Operations: Exact Rooms, Renewals, Payments & Contracts

Purpose

Last revised: 2026-07-10

This page defines the operational workflow for the combined pilot of วรเรสซิเดนซ์ / หอ 8 หลัง and หอพักวรอินเตอร์.

The product now focuses on exact-room inventory, early reservation, roommate grouping, SCB bulk payment documents, annual renewal, printed contracts, and university-system handoff.

Operational context

The two pilot dorm groups recruit before normal university payment rounds and manage many exact rooms with varying room configurations and prices. Staff currently repeat data entry across paper forms, Excel, bank/payment tools, and later university systems.

The MVP should reduce duplicate typing while preserving the real controls staff need:

  • exact-room visibility;
  • real payment commitment before permanent confirmation;
  • separate ROOM and HL accounting;
  • printed annual contracts;
  • traceable resident changes;
  • later official university handoff.

Core operational flow

configure campaign and exact rooms
→ renewal residents confirm/update information
→ remaining rooms open to new applicants
→ roommate groups or whole-room applicants choose exact rooms
→ short room-confirmation hold
→ 72-hour payment hold
→ finance exports SCB Excel batch
→ staff imports returned QR PDF
→ residents pay
→ staff imports SCB result report / records manual reference
→ all obligations complete
→ staff confirms exact room
→ contracts printed and signed
→ key handover recorded
→ confirmed residents queued for university handoff

1. Prepare campaign and inventory

Authorized staff configure:

  • dorm group;
  • buildings and floors;
  • globally unique exact rooms;
  • room dimensions/plan reference;
  • room configuration: normal, air-conditioned, HL, special/corner, etc.;
  • shared/whole-room capability;
  • pricing rules by action/year/occupancy;
  • renewal dates;
  • new-applicant dates;
  • roommate confirmation requirement;
  • 15-minute confirmation hold;
  • 72-hour payment hold;
  • contract template/version;
  • contact and policy notes.

2. Annual renewal

Renewal is core.

Staff opens a renewal campaign. Existing residents see prefilled data and confirm/update only what changed.

For shared rooms:

  • both residents may renew together;
  • leader may change when both confirm;
  • if one does not renew, the remaining resident receives a deadline to invite a replacement, buy out the room, move, or release it.

After renewal decisions, the exact-room availability list updates before new applications open.

3. New shared-room application

  • Both applicants complete profiles.
  • Leader sends roommate invitation.
  • Invitation expires after 48 hours.
  • Only one active group/invitation per resident.
  • Invited resident accepts before room selection.
  • Leader selects exact room and clicks Reserve.
  • Room is held immediately.
  • When campaign requires it, roommate confirms selected room within 15 minutes.
  • Failure releases room immediately.
  • Successful group moves to the 72-hour payment hold.

4. Whole-room application

  • One resident selects Whole Room / เหมาห้อง.
  • Second occupancy slot is blocked.
  • Full-room price is used.
  • One obligation per payment action and one contract are created.

5. Manual reservation

Authorized dorm staff may reserve on behalf of residents.

Required controls:

  • exact room and occupancy selected;
  • resident/group identified;
  • staff action is final and does not require resident confirmation;
  • mandatory reason;
  • audit event;
  • normal payment and contract workflow still applies unless explicitly overridden by permission.

6. Pricing and amount review

System calculates standard amounts.

Finance/payment staff review only:

  • manual override;
  • unusual room configuration;
  • resident-specific exception;
  • shared-to-whole conversion;
  • replacement/correction.

One-off override requires reason and affects only current obligation/export row. Permanent price changes use a separate versioned pricing action.

7. HL split billing

HL rooms create:

  • ROOM obligation for university room fee;
  • HL obligation for Happy Living/service-company portion.

Student-facing UX may call the room air-conditioned, but the backend and reports keep both actions separate.

8. SCB export

Finance/payment staff:

  • filter obligations ready for export;
  • review validation errors;
  • preview Excel rows;
  • generate .xlsx with worksheet SLIPS;
  • leave ID blank;
  • store the batch and exact rows;
  • upload the file to the existing SCB/internal application outside our app.

Current batch grouping permits all rows together when accepted, but remains configurable.

9. Returned QR PDF

SCB/internal software returns one combined multipage PDF.

Staff imports it into our app.

The app:

  • stores original PDF privately;
  • indexes pages;
  • extracts resident-specific text;
  • matches pages to obligations;
  • sends ambiguous pages to review;
  • never relies only on room number, action code, or page order.

10. Payment result

Normal path:

  • staff downloads SCB transaction/result report;
  • imports it into our app;
  • system matches results to obligations;
  • exceptions go to review.

Manual path:

  • authorized staff records bank transaction/reference and mandatory note.

Five-baht SCB Bill Payment fee is absorbed by dorm/company under the current baseline.

11. Reservation confirmation

Current provisional rule:

all required obligations for all group members paid
+ authorized staff confirmation
→ exact room confirmed

If one roommate pays and the other does not:

  • hold remains until shared deadline;
  • at expiry, release/cancel group under provisional rule;
  • paid amount enters manual/refund resolution.

Other applicants see the room as temporarily held until expiry.

12. Contract operations

After confirmation:

  • shared room generates two separate contracts under one reservation group;
  • whole-room generates one contract using same template with different occupancy/price fields;
  • applicant or staff may print;
  • roommates may sign separately;
  • shared group remains incomplete until both signed contracts are received;
  • signed scan/photo is required and stored privately;
  • physical photo/supporting paper may still be attached to printed contract according to dorm procedure;
  • reprint/correction requires reason and preserves prior versions.

13. Key handover

Key handover is a separate signed record linked to the contract.

Before handover, staff should verify:

  • room confirmed;
  • required payments complete;
  • contract signed/received;
  • required documents attached;
  • room/key conditions satisfied.

Exact checklist remains stakeholder data.

14. University handoff

Our app queues confirmed residents until the official university round opens.

Staff then exports the required handoff file or performs the approved process.

If roommate, room, or amount changes after submission:

  • do not overwrite original;
  • create correction record/export;
  • retain original and corrected history.

Operational edge cases

Invitation expires

Release invitation; no room was held before leader selected a room.

Room confirmation expires

Release exact room immediately after 15 minutes.

Payment hold expires

Release room/group exactly once and open payment/refund review when money exists.

One roommate paid

Do not permanently confirm group. Keep pending until shared deadline.

Shared to whole-room conversion

Recalculate; supersede old obligations; generate new full-room obligations; preserve history.

Roommate replacement

Staff/approved workflow supersedes old resident obligations/contracts and creates new ones.

Returned PDF page unmatched

Finance staff reviews extracted text and manually maps with mandatory reason.

Payment result unmatched

Send to exception queue; do not apply payment automatically.

Under/over/duplicate/late payment

Staff review. No silent auto-confirmation.

Fee-exempt occupant

Policy remains unresolved; do not improvise a zero bill without approval.

Always require for:

  • manual reservation;
  • manual payment;
  • amount override;
  • permanent pricing change;
  • room block/unblock;
  • cancellation/release override;
  • roommate replacement;
  • PDF page rematch/replacement;
  • payment exception resolution;
  • contract reprint/correction/cancellation;
  • university correction export.

Deferred scope

  • digital contract signing.
