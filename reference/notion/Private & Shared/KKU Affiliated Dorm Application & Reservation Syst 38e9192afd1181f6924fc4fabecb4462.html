Current product thesis

This system helps KKU affiliated dorms recruit and reserve residents earlier by moving paper/Line/manual application processes into a controlled online workflow with configurable payment rules, slip verification, room availability tracking, admin approval, and export/reporting.

Why this pivot happened

Stakeholder discussion showed that affiliated dorms do not simply follow central dorm booking rules.

Key points:

  1. Some affiliated dorms want residents to pay when applying or reserving.
  1. Payment conditions vary by dorm and applicant type.
  1. Some dorms may require damage deposit immediately.
  1. Some dorms may require first-year students to pay full 2 terms immediately when booking.
  1. หอ 8 หลัง / วรเรสซิเดนซ์ and หออินเตอร์ want to recruit earlier and fill rooms quickly.
  1. หอ 9 หลัง may have only a small portion using central booking; most rooms may prefer earlier recruitment.
  1. Invoice printing/payment documents may still go through the existing shared system, so core product should support export before full integration.
  1. Current residents who want to stay may reserve first; remaining rooms then open to others/new students.
  1. There is no fixed central-dorm-style freshman/senior quota unless each dorm defines one.

Important current decisions

Product decision

The system is not mainly a fairness allocation engine. It is an affiliated dorm online application and reservation management system.

Payment decision

Our app prepares resident payment obligations and exports the SCB-required Excel batch. The existing SCB/internal application generates the official QR payment forms and result report. Our app imports/matches those files, manages exceptions, and lets authorized staff confirm payment. Applicant slip upload and one shared static QR are not the normal path.

Applicant identity, exact-room, and contract decision

Applicants may begin with KKU SSO or a verified personal-email account. Both paths resolve to one internal user. KKU SSO remains optional.

Shared residents form a two-person group before exact-room selection; one resident may instead rent the whole room. After all required resident obligations are paid and staff confirms the reservation, the system generates printable contracts. Signed scans/photos and separate key handover are permission-controlled and audited.

Storage decision

Use SeaweedFS through its S3-compatible gateway for slips/documents. PostgreSQL stores metadata only. Backend code should use an S3-compatible client instead of writing directly to raw SeaweedFS/Filer endpoints.

Backend decision

Use GORM + sqlc:

  • GORM for normal CRUD and admin screens
  • sqlc for critical quota/reservation/payment verification/reporting queries

Deployment decision

Use Docker Compose and Nginx. No GitHub Actions in core product.
