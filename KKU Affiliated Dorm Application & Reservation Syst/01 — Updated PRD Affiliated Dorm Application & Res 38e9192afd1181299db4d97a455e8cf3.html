01 — Updated PRD: Affiliated Dorm Application & Reservation System

Purpose

This is the current product requirements document for the KKU Affiliated Dorm Application & Reservation System.

This page supersedes the old allocation-first PRD. Older ideas such as lottery allocation, priority scoring, and central-dorm-style fairness rules are now treated as future/optional unless stakeholders request them.

Product direction

This is a real operational product concept for affiliated dorms under KKU, not only a senior-project prototype.

The system should help affiliated dorms receive applications earlier than central dorm rounds, manage different payment conditions, verify payment slips, reserve rooms or room types, and track availability without relying mainly on paper forms, phone calls, Line chats, and manual spreadsheets.

Problem statement

Affiliated dorms need a flexible online workflow to recruit residents early, collect applications, require payment according to dorm-specific rules, verify payment slips, and confirm reservations while keeping room availability and reports organized.

Applicants need a clear way to know:

  • Which affiliated dorm campaigns are open
  • What room types are available
  • What must be paid at application or reservation time
  • Whether payment means damage deposit, 1 term, 2 terms, or another condition
  • How to submit an application online
  • How to upload payment slip
  • Whether the slip has been verified
  • Whether the reservation is confirmed
  • What to do next after approval

Dorm managers need a system that helps them fill rooms quickly while keeping records clean, payment evidence organized, and reservation status visible.

Product thesis

This system helps KKU affiliated dorms move from scattered manual recruitment to a controlled online reservation workflow with configurable payment rules, slip verification, room availability tracking, admin approval, and export/reporting for official processing.

What changed from the old concept

Old concept

The old concept focused on:

  • Fair allocation
  • Priority scoring
  • Lottery/tie-breaker rules
  • Waitlist transparency
  • Central-dorm-style allocation rounds

Current concept

The current concept focuses on:

  • Online application campaigns
  • Early recruitment by affiliated dorms
  • Payment conditions by dorm/applicant type
  • UniPay payment or manual evidence upload
  • Staff verification
  • Reservation confirmation
  • Room type quota tracking
  • Export to existing invoice/payment workflow

Updated product goal

Build an online application and reservation management system for KKU affiliated dorms that supports:

  1. Early application campaigns by dorm.
  1. Optional renewal-first reservation for current residents.
  1. Public application after remaining rooms are known.
  1. Configurable payment rules per dorm, room type, campaign, and applicant type.
  1. UniPay payment or manual evidence upload and staff verification.
  1. Reservation approval, rejection, cancellation, expiration, and status tracking.
  1. Room or room-type availability tracking.
  1. Export/reporting for existing invoice/payment process.
  1. Future integration with official KKU systems if access is granted.

Could have / future

  • Exact room selection
  • Roommate/group application
  • Automatic slip verification
  • Real payment gateway
  • KKU SSO integration
  • Existing invoice system integration
  • Maintenance reporting
  • Check-in workflow
  • Advanced allocation/fairness engine

Non-goals for core product release

The core product release should not try to:

  • Replace the official invoice system immediately
  • Integrate real payment gateway immediately
  • Force affiliated dorms into central dorm booking rules
  • Implement exact room selection before room-type quota is stable
  • Automate refunds
  • Launch all dorms at once without a pilot
  • Use AI to decide who gets a room

User roles

Applicant / student

Can:

  • View open affiliated dorm campaigns
  • View dorm and room type details
  • See payment conditions before applying
  • Submit online application
  • Upload payment slip
  • Track application/reservation status
  • See rejection reason and re-upload if allowed
  • Receive next-step instructions after approval

Current resident

Can:

  • Confirm intention to stay for next term/year if renewal is included
  • Reserve before public applicants if dorm policy allows
  • Upload required payment slip
  • Keep same room or request change if policy allows

Dorm manager / dorm admin

Can:

  • Create application campaigns
  • Set room type availability/quota
  • Set payment conditions
  • Review applications
  • Verify or reject slips
  • Approve or reject reservations
  • Manage cancellations and expirations
  • Export applicant/payment reports

Finance/admin staff

Can:

  • Review payment evidence
  • Confirm payment status
  • Export payment reports
  • Coordinate with existing invoice/payment workflow

Dorm service division admin / super admin

Can:

  • View all dorms
  • Manage system settings
  • Manage roles
  • Review reports
  • Monitor audit logs

Core workflow

Dorm manager opens application campaign
→ Admin sets room type availability and payment condition
→ Applicant views dorm/room type and payment condition
→ Applicant submits online form
→ System creates application and payment requirement
→ Applicant uploads payment slip
→ Staff verifies slip and applicant information
→ Admin approves or rejects reservation
→ Room count/status updates
→ Applicant tracks reservation status
→ Admin exports list/report for invoice or official processing

Key business rules to confirm

These are blocking questions for stakeholders:

  1. Which dorm should be the first pilot?
  1. Does the applicant choose exact room or only room type?
  1. What payment is required for each dorm/applicant type?
  1. Who verifies payment slips?
  1. Which status officially means “ได้ห้องแล้ว”?
  1. How long should payment/slip deadlines be?
  1. What happens if a slip is rejected?
  1. What happens if quota is full after applicant pays?
  1. What is the cancellation/refund policy?
  1. What export format does the existing invoice workflow need?
