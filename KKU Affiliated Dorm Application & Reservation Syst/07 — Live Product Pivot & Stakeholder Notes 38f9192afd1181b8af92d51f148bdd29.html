What changed from the original idea

Original idea

The original project focused on:

  • Fair allocation
  • Transparent waitlist
  • Priority scoring
  • Allocation engine
  • Admin dashboard for allocation

This is still useful, but it is not the main live need discovered.

New live need

The new product should focus on:

  • Online application instead of paper form
  • Payment required during application/reservation
  • Uploading payment slip
  • Staff verification of slip
  • Reservation confirmation
  • Room/room-type availability tracking
  • Early recruitment campaign management
  • Export/report for official process

Stakeholder Q&A summary

Question 1 — payment at application/reservation time

Summary:

A stakeholder asked whether applicants need to pay when applying/reserving, causing paper form submission at the dorm.

Answer summary:

The dorms can have varied conditions, such as:

  • Requiring damage deposit immediately
  • Requiring first-year students to pay full amount for 2 terms immediately when booking

Product implication:

  • Payment conditions must be configurable.
  • The system cannot assume one universal payment rule.
  • The application page must clearly show payment requirement before submission.
  • Slip upload and verification are central core product release features.

Question 2 — online application and UniPay payment or manual evidence upload

Summary:

The idea of online application and payment UniPay payment or manual evidence upload was raised.

Answer summary:

Initial discussion with managers of หอ 8 หลัง and หออินเตอร์ shows interest in an online booking/application website, and they are willing to discuss details with students.

Product implication:

  • Stakeholders are open to the idea.
  • The next meeting should be used to define details, not just pitch a vague concept.
  • Prepare prototype flows around online application + UniPay payment or manual evidence upload + admin verification.

Question 3 — existing booking and invoice systems

Summary:

Research found central dorm online booking and invoice printing. The question was whether central booking covers หอส่วนกลาง 1–27 and หอ 9 หลัง, but not หอ 8 หลัง and หออินเตอร์, and whether all invoice printing happens through the same system.

Answer summary:

  • หอ 8 หลัง + อินเตอร์ do not want to book through the same central flow because they want to recruit before normal central dorm rounds.
  • They want to fill rooms quickly.
  • หอ 9 หลัง has only a small portion joining central booking; most want earlier recruitment for the same reason.
  • Invoice printing goes through the same system for all.

Product implication:

  • The new system should not replace central booking completely.
  • The new system supports affiliated dorm early recruitment and reservation.
  • Invoice/payment document process may remain external at first.
  • core product release should support export to existing invoice system.

Question 4 — booking order for หอ 8 หลัง + อินเตอร์

Summary:

The question compared affiliated dorm booking with central dorm booking, where central dorm allows first-year booking and reserves some room proportion for seniors.

Answer summary:

  • Affiliated dorms do not follow that model.
  • Current residents who want to stay continue/reserve first.
  • Remaining vacant rooms are opened to general applicants, including seniors from other dorms and new students.
  • Usually rooms fill very fast, sometimes by quota-round freshmen.
  • No rooms are reserved in advance for freshmen.
  • Central dorms reserve proportions from the beginning, such as seniors 40% and freshmen 60%, and freshman rounds are split into Portfolio / Quota / Admission.

Product implication:

  • The system should support renewal-first workflow.
  • The system should not enforce central dorm quota logic.
  • The system should allow managers to open early campaigns.
  • Room availability must update after renewals.
  • Public application should use remaining availability.

Updated product thesis

Affiliated dorms need a flexible online workflow to recruit residents early, collect applications, require payment according to dorm-specific rules, verify payment slips, and confirm reservations while keeping room availability and reports organized.

Updated core product release in one sentence

A web system where dorm managers open campaigns, applicants apply online and upload payment slips, staff verify payment evidence, and the system confirms reservations while tracking remaining room availability.

What should not be overbuilt first

Do not make these the center of the live core product release:

  • Complex priority scoring
  • Full lottery allocation
  • Automatic payment gateway
  • Exact room selection
  • Deep integration with central systems
  • Maintenance module
  • Chatbot
  • Mobile app

These can be future versions.

What should be built first

Build these first:

  1. Campaign setup
  1. Dorm/room type availability
  1. Online application form
  1. Payment condition display
  1. Slip upload
  1. Slip verification dashboard
  1. Reservation approval/rejection
  1. Reservation status tracker
  1. Export/report
  1. Audit log

Biggest uncertainty

The biggest uncertainty is the official reservation confirmation rule.

Need to clarify:

  • Is reservation confirmed after application submission?
  • After payment?
  • After UniPay payment or manual evidence upload?
  • After staff verification?
  • After invoice system record?

Recommended product assumption:

Reservation is confirmed only after staff verifies required payment evidence.

Product positioning for stakeholders

Use this wording:

We are not trying to force affiliated dorms into the central dorm booking process. We are designing a flexible system for affiliated dorms to manage their own early recruitment rounds, payment conditions, slip verification, and reservation tracking in a more organized online workflow.
