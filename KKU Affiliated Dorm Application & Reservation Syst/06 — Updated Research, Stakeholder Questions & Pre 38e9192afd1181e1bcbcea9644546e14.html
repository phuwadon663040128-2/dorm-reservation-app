Purpose

This page prepares the team for real stakeholder discussion and presentation of the KKU Affiliated Dorm Application & Reservation System.

It replaces the older allocation-first presentation kit. The current story is: affiliated dorm early recruitment, online application, payment rules, slip verification, reservation confirmation, room type quota, and export/reporting.

Stakeholder facts from latest discussion

Dorms in scope

  • หอพักนพรัตน์ / หอพัก 9 หลัง
  • หอพักสวัสดิการนักศึกษา / วรเรสซิเดนซ์
  • หอพักวรอินเตอร์

Stakeholders

  • ผอ. อ้อม — รักษาการแทนผู้อำนวยการกองบริการหอพักนักศึกษา
  • อาจารย์กานดา — อาจารย์ที่ปรึกษาหลัก
  • ผู้จัดการหอพักในกำกับ

Operational facts to reflect

  • Payment can be required at application/reservation time.
  • Conditions vary by dorm.
  • Some dorms may require damage deposit immediately.
  • Some dorms may require first-year students to pay full 2 terms immediately when booking.
  • หอ 8 หลัง / วรเรสซิเดนซ์ and หออินเตอร์ want early recruitment and are interested in an online booking/application website.
  • หอ 8 หลัง / อินเตอร์ do not want to join the central dorm booking timing because they want applicants before the normal central dorm rounds.
  • หอ 9 หลัง may use central booking for only a small portion of rooms; most rooms may prefer earlier recruitment.
  • Invoice printing/payment document process may still be through the existing shared system.
  • Current residents who want to stay continue first, then remaining rooms are open to others/new students.
  • No fixed freshman room holding like the central dorm model unless a dorm manager defines it.

Research objective

Main research objective:

Understand how affiliated dorms currently receive applications, collect payment, verify evidence, confirm reservations, manage remaining rooms, and coordinate with existing invoice/payment systems.

Updated presentation positioning

Use this positioning:

Our project is an online application and reservation management system for KKU affiliated dorms. These dorms have different operational needs from central dorms because they want to recruit residents earlier and may require payment at application/reservation time. Our system supports campaign setup, online application, payment rule display, UniPay payment or manual evidence upload, staff verification, reservation confirmation, room availability tracking, file storage with SeaweedFS, and export/reporting for official processing.

Updated demo script

Scene 1 — Applicant applies

  1. Applicant opens campaign page.
  1. Applicant chooses dorm/room type.
  1. System shows payment requirement.
  1. Applicant submits form.
  1. Applicant uploads slip.
  1. Applicant sees status: waiting for verification.

Narration:

Unlike a simple booking website, this flow clearly shows the payment condition before the applicant submits. The reservation is not confirmed until staff verifies the slip.

Scene 2 — Admin verifies

  1. Dorm manager opens dashboard.
  1. Manager sees slips waiting for verification.
  1. Manager opens applicant detail.
  1. Manager checks slip and expected amount.
  1. Manager approves reservation.
  1. Remaining room quota updates.

Narration:

This helps dorm managers fill rooms quickly while keeping payment evidence and reservation records organized.

Scene 3 — Export

  1. Admin opens report page.
  1. Filters approved reservations.
  1. Exports list for official invoice/payment workflow.

Narration:

If full integration with the existing invoice system is not available at first, the core product release can still support export for official processing.
